=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
How far can your general knowledge take you? Answer 15 questions in a row, climb the prize ladder and reach 1,000,000 points in Road to a Million, the TV-style trivia quiz game.

🎯 THE GAME
• 15 questions, from easy to expert
• Safe havens at questions 5 and 10 protect your points
• A countdown timer, suspense music and the "final answer?" moment
• Walk away with your points at any time… or risk it all

🆘 LIFELINES
• 50:50: remove two wrong answers
• Ask the audience: see what the crowd thinks
• Phone a friend: get a tip (but is your friend sure?)
• Switch: swap the question for a new one
• Second chance: wrong answer? Watch a short video and keep playing

📚 1,000+ QUESTIONS ON 10 TOPICS
General knowledge • Science • History • Geography • Sport • Religion and Islamic civilisation • Technology and programming • Literature and language • Film and TV • The Maghreb (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia…)

Mix all topics or pick your favourite. Every answer comes with a short explanation, so you learn something new in every game. Questions you have already seen are set aside, so you rarely get the same one twice.

❓ CAN YOU ANSWER THESE?
• What is the name of the famous square in the heart of Marrakesh?
• In which city is the University of al-Qarawiyyin?
• Which commander founded the city of Kairouan?
• In which mountain range is Mount Toubkal, the highest peak in North Africa?
• In which year was the first football World Cup held?
The answers (and 1,000 more questions) are waiting for you in the game.

🏆 PLAY WITH OTHERS
• World ranking: compare your best score with players around the world
• Friends challenge: create a challenge, send the code, and your friends answer exactly the same 15 questions. Who will get the furthest?

🌍 3 LANGUAGES
Play in English, French or Arabic, and switch at any time.

✨ ALSO
• Play offline: all the questions are in the app
• Sound effects, music and vibration, each one can be turned off
• Spotted a mistake? Report the question in one tap

A fun brain game to test your culture, learn new facts and challenge your family and friends. Free to play, supported by ads.
